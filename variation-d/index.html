What is Coconut Stock?

More than a coconut:
it's wearing your name

We take young Thai coconuts, trim each one by hand into its diamond shape and heat-brand it with your logo, monogram or names. A drink, a keepsake and a brand moment in one.

How it works

From the grove
to their hands.
No stress.

Start your order
01

Share your details and logo

Tell us the date, the place and how many coconuts, and send your artwork. Any file works to start; we'll ask for a vector before we brand.

02

Approve your preview

Our design team fits your logo to the 3″ × 2.2″ brand area and sends a preview. Nothing is branded until you say yes.

03

We brand and prepare

CNC machinery and a heat-burn method give a clean, permanent mark on every coconut, trimmed and chilled for the day.

04

Delivered, opened and served

Local delivery in our five markets or nationwide shipping. Add opening, serving or a coconut cart if you want the full show.
