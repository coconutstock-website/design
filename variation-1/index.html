FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still wondering about something? Ask us in the quote form ›

What is the minimum order?

Add minimum order Smaller orders can usually ship nationwide.

How far in advance should I order?

Add lead time For weddings and large events, reach out as soon as you have a date.

What logo file do you need?

A vector file: EPS, SVG or AI. If you only have a PNG or JPG, send it anyway and our design team will check it with you before anything is branded.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver locally in Miami and South Florida, Orlando, Tampa, Houston and The Bahamas. Anywhere else in the US, we ship nationwide.

Can your team serve at our event?

Yes. Add opening and serving or a coconut cart when you request a quote.

Do you supply hotels on a schedule?

Yes. The hospitality program is recurring delivery for hotels, restaurants and clubs, with your logo on every coconut.
