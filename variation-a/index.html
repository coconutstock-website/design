What is Coconut Stock?

A fresh coconut, *wearing* your name

We take young Thai Nam Hom coconuts, trim each one by hand into its signature diamond shape, and heat-brand it with your logo, monogram or names. It is a drink, a keepsake and a brand moment in one, served at weddings, hotel pools and launches.
