A fresh coconutwearing your name

We take young Thai Nam Hom coconuts, trim each one by hand into its signature diamond shape, and heat-brand it with your logo, monogram or names. A drink, a keepsake and a brand moment in one.

Good to know

What is the minimum order?

Add minimum order Smaller orders can usually ship nationwide.

How far ahead should I order?

Add lead time For weddings and large events, reach out as soon as you have a date.

What logo file do you need?

A vector file: EPS, SVG or AI. Only have a PNG or JPG? Send it anyway and our design team will check it with you first.

We deliver in Miami and South Florida, Orlando, Tampa, Houston and The Bahamas, and ship nationwide across the US.
